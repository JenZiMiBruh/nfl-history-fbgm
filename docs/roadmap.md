# Timeline & roadmap

What's out, how it stays current, and what's coming. Like Madden, the league gets **roster updates all year**: every Sunday during the season, and at every big offseason moment. Dates for future items are targets, not promises.

## Released

| Date | Version | What |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 1, 2026 | **v1.0** | 1966 to today: 28,700+ real players, ratings, 178 logo & jersey eras, real schedules, 121 real events, the real 2027–2029 draft classes, the season selector and the mod |
| Nov 2, 2026 | **Update** | Real-roster leagues fixed: AI teams no longer trade with each other or sign players who weren't really theirs; strays are released each preseason. New site look, faster season slider |

## The yearly update cycle

Every year follows the same schedule, the way Madden's roster updates do.

| When | Update | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Every Sunday, Sep–Feb** | Weekly roster update | Stats, signings, trades, injuries and ratings, through the Super Bowl |
| **After the Super Bowl, Feb** | Season wrap-up | Final stats and awards added to history; the season becomes pickable in the season selector |
| **Mid-Mar** | Free agency update | Re-signings, cuts, trades and free-agent signings on their new teams. Retirements |
| **Late Apr** | Draft update | The real draft: every pick on the team that made it. Next three draft classes re-ranked |
| **May** | Schedule update | The real new schedule, game for game, as soon as the NFL releases it |
| **Late Aug** | **New season release** | Full preseason league: 53-man rosters after cuts, fresh ratings from last season's production, new uniforms and logos, ready for Week 1 |

## Coming to the mod

The features at the top of the list, in rough order:

| | Feature | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🏆 | **Named Super Bowls, forever** | Every title game gets its real name: Super Bowl I in January 1967 through LX, then the numbering keeps going in your league (LXI, LXII … C) with Roman numerals, a host city and a date. The 1966–69 AFL and NFL Championship Games keep their real names too. |
| 📅 | **A real NFL calendar** | Games land on real days and dates, not just “Week 9”: Thursday, Sunday and Monday, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Saturdays late in the season and the playoffs on their real weekends. |
| 🌙 | **Named game slots** | Monday Night Football (from 1970), Sunday Night Football, Thursday Night Football, Thanksgiving in Detroit and Dallas, the Christmas games, Saturday doubleheaders. Each era only has the slots it really had. |
| 🕐 | **Kickoff times** | Early (1:00 ET), late afternoon (4:05/4:25), primetime (8:15) and London morning games, so you can see whether a game is midday, under the lights or at breakfast. |
| ⭐ | **Game of the Week** | The biggest matchup each week gets flagged and flexed into primetime, like the NFL does with Sunday night flex scheduling. |
