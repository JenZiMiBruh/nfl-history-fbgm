03Full experience

The mod

Everything above, plus history that keeps happening: the real schedule every season, real drafts, careers that follow the real one, real retirements, comebacks and suspensions, with a setting for each. It runs on your computer; your phone can play on it over Wi-Fi.

4

Play on your phone too

Leave the window open. On your phone, on the same Wi-Fi, open the address it shows (like http://192.168.1.20:3000). Your phone keeps its own leagues.

192.168.1.20:3000
Football GM with the NFL History mod, on your phone.
Android says “This site can't be reached”? Newer Android versions block Chrome from home-network addresses until you allow it: open Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions, set Nearby devices (or Local network) to Allow, then close Chrome fully and open the address again.
Next time, just double-click Install_and_Play again. It skips the setup, checks for updates and opens the game in seconds.
Is there an Android app? A native Android version exists: Football GM's engine inside a real app, no browser. Football GM's license doesn't allow sharing builds, so it can't be released, but I'd love to put it out someday.
