Double-click Install_and_Play

On Windows, double-click Install_and_Play.bat (on Mac or Linux, run install_and_play.sh). The first time, it installs what it needs and builds the game. That takes 5–10 minutes. After that it starts in seconds.

Play on your phone too

Leave the window open. On your phone, on the same Wi-Fi, open the address it shows (like http://192.168.1.20:3000). Your phone keeps its own leagues.

Android and "This site can't be reached"? Newer Android versions block Chrome from home-network addresses until you allow it: open Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions, set Nearby devices (or Local network) to Allow, then close Chrome fully and open the address again.

Yes, there's an Android app

A native Android version exists: Football GM's engine running inside a real app, no browser. Football GM's license doesn't allow distributing builds, so it can't be released, but I'd love to put it out someday.
