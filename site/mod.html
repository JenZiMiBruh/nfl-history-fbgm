01Real Player Determinism

One slider from random to real

RPD decides how closely careers follow the real ones. At 100%, Jerry Rice plays into his 40s and Barry Sanders walks away at 30. At 0%, everyone develops like a normal Football GM player. Anywhere in between mixes the two.

04Mod vs. regular Football GM

The Browns move to Baltimore

After 1995, the Browns moved to Baltimore. The NFL kept their name and history in Cleveland, so Baltimore started as the Ravens, and the Browns came back in 1999.

With the mod: the players, contracts and draft picks go to Baltimore with the team, like they really did. Cleveland keeps its history and returns in 1999.

Regular Football GM: it can't move a team without moving its history. So history stays correct in Cleveland, but the 1995 Browns become free agents and Baltimore gets an expansion draft.

We kept the history right instead of giving the Browns' records to Baltimore. Only leagues that start in 1995 or earlier are affected.
